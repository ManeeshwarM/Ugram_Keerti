The Sacred Rituals

The traditional steps of a Telugu wedding, from the groom's arrival to the evening star.

1

Snathakam

స్నాతకం

The groom completes his life as a student and prepares to enter family life.

2

Kasi Yatra

కాశీ యాత్ర

The groom playfully sets out for Kashi, and the bride's brother persuades him to stay and marry.

3

Edurukolu

ఎదురుకోలు

The two families meet and welcome each other with honour.

4

Kanyadanam

కన్యాదానం

Sri Venkataramana and Smt. Sujatha give their daughter's hand to the groom.

5

Jeelakarra Bellam

జీలకర్ర బెల్లం

At the exact muhurtham, bride and groom place cumin and jaggery on each other's heads. This is the moment of marriage.

7:53 AM
6

Mangalya Dharanam

మాంగల్య ధారణ

The groom ties the sacred mangalasutram with three knots.

7

Talambralu

తలంబ్రాలు

The couple shower each other with turmeric rice, the most joyful moment of the ceremony.

8

Saptapadi

సప్తపది

Seven steps around the sacred fire, one promise with each step.

9

Arundhati Darshanam

అరుంధతీ దర్శనం

The couple are shown the Arundhati star, the symbol of lifelong devotion.

10

Vindu

విందు

The wedding feast for all guests at the venue.

From 12 Noon
